# Salon Queue – Brief

## Problem
On weekends customers sit 1–2 hours in the salon without knowing their turn.
Some leave, others argue about who came first. The receptionist keeps
answering "how long will it take?".

## Users
Customers, receptionist/owner, barbers. Plus a TV in the waiting area.
Demo salon: Kesh Studio (10 AM – 9 PM), barbers Ravi, Amit, Imran.

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from your phone (QR at the door): name, phone, service(s), barber or 'Any'
2. Each barber shows their own wait time before you pick
3. Live status: token, position, estimated wait, 'Start coming' alert, 'It's your turn' screen
4. Receptionist dashboard: add walk-ins, call next, mark no-show, cancel
5. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done, On break
6. Waiting-area TV: who's with which barber + next 5 tokens, QR to join
7. Owner settings: services (time + price), barbers on duty, closing time, open/close queue, today's stats
8. English/Gujarati toggle, and a staff PIN for reception, barber and admin screens

## Don't build
Online payment, appointments for later dates, SMS/WhatsApp/push notifications,
OTP login, customer accounts, loyalty, multiple branches.

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel in navy + white: the token looks like a wallet pass,
soft white cards, light big numbers. TV and 'your turn' screen in dark navy.
Mobile-first. Labels in English and Gujarati.
